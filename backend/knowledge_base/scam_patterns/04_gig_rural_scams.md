# Scam Patterns Targeting Gig Workers and Rural Users in India

## Why Gig Workers and Rural Users Are Disproportionately Targeted
- Higher financial stress makes promises of quick money more compelling
- Less experience with formal financial products means less ability to recognize legitimate vs fake offers
- Lower digital literacy (especially around URL verification, app authentication)
- Social trust networks exploited — scams spread through WhatsApp family groups
- Less likely to report fraud due to fear of embarrassment or distrust of police

---

## 1. Fake Delivery Job Scams (Targeting Gig Economy)
**How it works:** A fake recruiter contacts you via WhatsApp or OLX claiming to offer delivery jobs with high earnings at Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, Blinkit, etc. They ask you to:
- Pay a "registration fee" of ₹500–₹2,000 to get your kit or uniform
- Complete an "onboarding fee" via UPI
- Share your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank details for "salary account setup"

**Reality:** Legitimate gig platforms NEVER charge registration fees. Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon Flex — none charge upfront money. You apply directly on their official apps.

**What's stolen:** Your registration fee AND your personal documents for identity fraud.

---

## 2. Fake Government Benefit / DBT Scams
**How it works:** A caller claims you are eligible for a government Direct Benefit Transfer (PM Kisan, PM Awas Yojana, MNREGA payment, COVID relief) but your "link is broken." They ask you to:
- Share your Aadhaar number + OTP to "re-link"
- Pay a small "processing fee" via UPI to complete the linkage
- Install an app to "fix" the connectivity

**Reality:** DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) happens automatically to your linked bank account. No government scheme requires you to call a helpline or pay a fee to receive your entitled benefit.

**Verify your actual PM Kisan status:** pmkisan.gov.in (check with your registered Aadhaar/mobile)

---

## 3. Fake Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Agents
**How it works:** Fraudsters visit villages claiming to be bank or government agents offering to help farmers get Kisan Credit Cards (KCC) for loans up to ₹3 lakh. They collect:
- ₹500–₹2,000 as "processing fee"
- Thumb impressions on blank forms (biometric ID theft)
- Bank passbook for "verification"

**Reality:** Kisan Credit Card applications are processed at bank branches for FREE. No legitimate agent takes fees or collects blank forms with thumb impressions outside a bank.

---

## 4. Property Fraud in Rural Areas
**How it works:**
- Fake registration agents offer to register land/property documents for lower fees
- Fraudulent buyers pay with fake cheques or manipulated bank statements
- Fake NOC (No Objection Certificate) sellers claiming property is free of dues

**Protection:** All land registration in India must happen at the Sub-Registrar's Office. Verify property ownership records on state government portals (e.g., Mahabhulekh for Maharashtra, Bhoomi for Karnataka, Bhunaksha for UP).

---

## 5. Fake Loan Waiver Scams
**How it works:** Following government announcements of farm loan waivers, fraudsters call farmers claiming to process the waiver "faster" for a small fee, or that the farmer is disqualified unless they pay a fine.

**Reality:** Loan waivers are processed directly between the bank and the state government. No payment is required. Banks write off qualifying loans automatically.

---

## 6. Instant Loan App Targeting Low-Income Users
Particularly predatory apps targeting gig workers and low-income earners:
- Offer ₹5,000–₹50,000 loans "in 5 minutes" with no documentation
- Effective annualized interest rate: 150–500%
- Access to phone contacts; if payment missed, send threatening/defamatory messages to all contacts

**RBI action:** RBI has created a whitelist of approved digital lenders. Unregistered apps operate illegally. RBI's "Sachet" portal allows public to check if an entity is registered: sachet.rbi.org.in
