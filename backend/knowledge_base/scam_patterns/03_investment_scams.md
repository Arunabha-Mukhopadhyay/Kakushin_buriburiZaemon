# Investment Scams on Social Media and Online Platforms

## 1. Fake Stock Market Tips and "Operator" Stocks
**How it works:** Scammers create WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, or YouTube channels posing as "SEBI-registered analysts" or "expert traders." They provide a few accurate stock tips (often obvious calls) to build trust, then recommend a specific obscure stock. The scammer has already bought this stock cheaply; as victims buy, the price rises; the scammer sells at the peak ("pump and dump"), leaving victims with losses.

**Red flags:**
- "Free" stock tips on WhatsApp/Telegram groups you were added to
- Claims of 200–500% returns in weeks
- Tips on penny stocks or obscure small caps
- "Limited slots" or "join before midnight" pressure
- Advice to buy immediately without explaining fundamentals

**Legal fact:** All investment advice must come from a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA). Verify at sebi.gov.in > Intermediaries > Investment Advisers.

## 2. Fake Mutual Fund Apps and Cloned Websites
**How it works:** Fraudsters create fake apps or websites that closely mimic legitimate AMCs (Asset Management Companies) like SBI Mutual Fund, HDFC AMC, or Mirae Asset. They show fake "portfolio values" growing rapidly. Victims invest real money that is never invested — it goes to the fraudster. When victims try to withdraw, they are asked to pay "exit loads," "tax clearances," or "verification fees."

**Red flags:**
- App not on official Google Play Store or Apple App Store
- Website URL slightly different from official (e.g., "hdfcmf-invest.com" instead of "hdfcfund.com")
- No AMFI registration number displayed
- Promised returns of 30–50% per year "guaranteed"

**Verify:** All mutual funds in India must be registered with AMFI. Check amfiindia.com for the registered AMC list. Only invest through official AMC websites, registered platforms like Zerodha Coin, Groww (verify on SEBI), or your bank.

## 3. Cryptocurrency and NFT Scams
**How it works:** India has seen a surge in crypto scams. Common patterns:
- **Fake exchanges:** Fraudulent platforms that look like real crypto exchanges; you deposit money, cannot withdraw
- **Celebrity endorsement deepfakes:** Fake videos of Elon Musk, PM Modi, Mukesh Ambani promoting "guaranteed" crypto returns
- **Crypto giveaway scams:** "Send 1 ETH, get 2 ETH back" — always a scam
- **Rug pulls:** Project raises funds, team disappears
- **Romance/Pig Butchering scam:** Online romantic relationship built over weeks/months; then "friend" convinces victim to invest in their crypto platform

**Legal status in India (2025):** Crypto is not a legal tender but is taxable. 30% flat tax on gains + 1% TDS. No regulatory body currently oversees crypto exchanges the way SEBI oversees stock exchanges — buyer beware.

---

## 4. Fraudulent Portfolio Management Services (PMS)
**How it works:** Unregistered individuals claim to manage your portfolio for a fee, promising extraordinary returns. They take custody of your money or ask you to transfer funds to them directly.

**Legal fact:** SEBI requires a minimum AUM of ₹50 crore and strict registration for Portfolio Management Services. Anyone offering PMS without SEBI registration is operating illegally.

**Verify:** sebi.gov.in > Intermediaries > Portfolio Managers

---

## 5. Chit Fund Scams
**How it works:** Legitimate chit funds are registered under the Chit Funds Act 1982. Fraudulent operators run unregistered chit funds that collapse when organizers abscond with collected money.

**Verify:** A registered chit fund must display its registration certificate and be verifiable with the State Registrar of Chit Funds.

**Red flags:**
- No registered office or certificate
- Promised dividends that are unrealistically high
- Organizer is an individual, not a registered company

---

## SEBI SCORES — Investor Complaint Portal
If you have been mis-sold a financial product or defrauded by a SEBI-regulated entity:
- **Portal:** scores.sebi.gov.in
- **Helpline:** 1800-266-7575 (toll-free)
- SEBI must resolve registered complaints within 30 working days

## Summary: Verify Before You Invest
| Entity Type | Verify At |
|---|---|
| Mutual Funds | amfiindia.com |
| Stock Brokers | sebi.gov.in or nseindia.com |
| Investment Advisers | sebi.gov.in > Intermediaries |
| NBFCs (loan apps) | rbi.org.in > Regulated Entities |
| Insurance | irdai.gov.in |
| Chit Funds | State Registrar of Chit Funds |
