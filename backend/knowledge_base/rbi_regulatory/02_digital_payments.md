# RBI Regulations on Digital Payments — UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS

## UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

### What is UPI?
UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) that allows instant fund transfer between bank accounts via mobile apps. Regulated by RBI and NPCI.

### Transaction Limits (as of 2025)
- **Standard UPI:** ₹1 lakh per transaction
- **UPI for verified merchants:** ₹2 lakh per transaction
- **UPI for specific categories (IPO, RBI retail direct, medical/education):** ₹5 lakh per transaction
- No limit on number of transactions per day (subject to bank's own policies)

### Failed Transaction — Refund Rules
Per RBI circular (DPSS.CO.PD No.629/02.01.014/2019-20):
- If debit occurs but payee not credited: Auto-refund within **T+1 business day** (next working day)
- If bank delays beyond T+1: Bank must pay compensation at **savings account interest rate** per day
- If UPI times out: Refund within 5 business days

### Customer Rights in UPI Fraud
- **Unauthorized transaction reported within 3 working days:** Zero liability (if bank's system was compromised)
- **Customer shared credentials (OTP/PIN):** Customer bears liability
- Dispute filing: Through your bank app → "Report an issue" or directly at npci.org.in

## NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)

### How it Works
NEFT processes transactions in half-hourly batches, 24x7x365 (from December 2019). Funds transferred between bank accounts across India.

### Limits
- **Minimum:** ₹1 (no minimum in practice)
- **Maximum:** No upper limit set by RBI (banks may set their own)
- **Cost:** Free for savings account holders at most banks (RBI directive 2019)

### Refund on Failure
- If NEFT fails: Full refund to sender's account within **2 hours** of batch settlement
- If refund delayed: Sender entitled to interest at current RBI repo rate

---

## RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)

### How it Works
RTGS is for large-value, time-critical transactions. Each transaction settles individually and instantly (real-time).

### Limits
- **Minimum:** ₹2 lakh
- **Maximum:** No upper limit set by RBI
- **Available:** 24x7x365 (since December 2020)
- **Cost:** Free (RBI made RTGS/NEFT charges free in 2019)

---

## IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)

### How it Works
IMPS enables instant fund transfer 24x7 including holidays, using mobile number + MMID or account number + IFSC.

### Limits
- **Maximum:** ₹5 lakh per transaction
- **Cost:** Banks may charge up to ₹15 per transaction (varies)

## Cheque-Related Regulations

### Positive Pay System (PPS)
For cheques above ₹50,000, banks have implemented Positive Pay — the account holder must confirm cheque details (date, amount, payee) via net banking or mobile app before the cheque is presented. This prevents cheque fraud.

### CTS (Cheque Truncation System)
All cheques in India are processed digitally via CTS. Physical cheques are scanned; the image is transmitted for clearing. Cheques must be CTS-2010 compliant (no staple holes, clear MICR band).
