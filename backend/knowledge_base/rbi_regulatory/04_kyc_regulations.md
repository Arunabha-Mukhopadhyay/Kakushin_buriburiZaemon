# RBI Regulations on KYC — Know Your Customer Rules for Bank Customers

## What is KYC?
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a regulatory process mandated by RBI under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA). Banks and financial institutions must verify the identity and address of every customer before providing services.

## Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) for KYC
RBI lists the following as Officially Valid Documents — any ONE is sufficient:
1. **Passport**
2. **Driving Licence**
3. **Proof of possession of Aadhaar number** (Aadhaar card / e-Aadhaar)
4. **Voter ID (Election Commission card)**
5. **NREGA Job Card** (signed by a State Government officer)
6. **National Population Register letter** (with name and address)
7. **PAN Card** (for identity; must be accompanied by an address proof OVD)

## Aadhaar-Based KYC (e-KYC) — Fastest Method
- Instant account opening using biometric authentication or OTP
- Aadhaar e-KYC is legally valid for all bank account openings, loan applications, mutual fund KYC, and insurance
- Banks cannot force you to submit physical documents if you consent to Aadhaar e-KYC

## Periodic KYC Update — Your Rights
Banks are required to do periodic KYC updates. RBI's rules on this:

### Customer Risk Categories
| Risk Category | KYC Update Frequency |
|---|---|
| Low risk | Every 10 years |
| Medium risk | Every 8 years |
| High risk | Every 2 years |

Most regular savings account holders are **low or medium risk** — KYC updates are infrequent.

### Important Rights During KYC Update
- Bank must give **prior notice** before restricting your account for KYC non-compliance
- Bank **cannot restrict** basic operations (credits, cheque payments) without proper notice
- If you have submitted KYC documents but bank hasn't processed: Account cannot be restricted
- Bank must give a **reasonable time** (typically 3 months) to complete KYC update

### Partial Freeze vs Full Freeze
- **Partial freeze:** Only debits blocked; credits (receiving money) remain active
- **Full freeze:** Only if KYC remains incomplete after repeated notices (rare for regular customers)

## Video-Based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP)
From 2020, RBI allows banks to complete KYC via live video call:
- Customer speaks to a bank officer on video
- Shows original documents to camera
- Facial match done with Aadhaar photo
- Eliminates the need to visit bank branch for account opening

Available through most private banks' apps (HDFC, ICICI, Axis) and increasingly at public banks.

## KYC for Mutual Funds — KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs)
Mutual fund KYC is centralized through KRAs (CAMS KRA, KFintech, NSE KRA, CSDL Ventures). Complete KYC once with any AMC/broker — it is valid across all mutual funds in India.
- Check KYC status: cvlkra.com or camskra.com

## What Banks CANNOT Do
- Refuse to open a basic savings account solely because you do not have a PAN card (alternate OVD is acceptable)
- Demand documents not on the OVD list
- Discriminate against applicants on basis of income level, profession, or place of origin
- Freeze accounts without adequate notice and opportunity to submit documents
