# RBI Consumer Protection — Your Rights as a Bank Customer

## Overview
The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has established a comprehensive framework to protect bank customers. Every customer of a bank regulated by RBI has legally enforceable rights.

## Right to Information
- Banks must provide a **Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC)** document before opening any account or issuing any loan/credit card
- Interest rates on loans must be disclosed as **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)** — the total cost including all fees
- Banks must provide a **Key Fact Statement (KFS)** for all retail and MSME loans from April 2024 onward
- Credit card statements must clearly show minimum amount due, total amount due, and interest-free period

## RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RBI IOS)
If your bank does not resolve your complaint within **30 days**, you can escalate to the RBI Ombudsman — a free, no-cost grievance redressal mechanism.

**What complaints are covered:**
- Unauthorized/fraudulent transactions
- Non-credit of funds to account
- Failure to refund money after failed transaction
- Mis-selling of insurance or investment products
- Non-issuance of acknowledgment / receipt for deposits
- Excessive/unauthorized charges and fees
- Non-adherence to fair lending practices

**How to file:**
- Online: cms.rbi.org.in (Complaint Management System)
- Email: crpc@rbi.org.in
- Phone: 14448 (Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre)
- Physical: Send written complaint to any RBI office

**Timeline:** RBI Ombudsman must dispose of complaints within 30 days of receiving complete information.

## Protection Against Unauthorized Transactions
RBI's circular on **Limited Liability of Customers** (July 2017, updated 2021):

| Cause of Fraud | Customer Liability |
|---|---|
| Negligence by bank | ZERO — full amount to be refunded |
| Third-party fraud (no customer fault) | ZERO if reported within 3 working days |
| Shared credentials (customer fault) | Customer bears full loss |

**Reporting window:**
- Report to bank within **3 working days** = Zero liability (if bank at fault)
- 4–7 days = Liability capped at ₹5,000–₹10,000 depending on account type
- Beyond 7 days = Full liability may apply

**How to report:** Call bank's 24x7 helpline immediately. Follow up in writing (email/branch visit) with transaction details.

## Fair Practices Code for Lenders
All RBI-regulated lenders must follow the Fair Practices Code (FPC):
- No coercive recovery practices
- Clear disclosure of all charges before loan disbursement
- No prepayment penalty on floating rate retail loans (RBI circular 2012)
- Loan agreement in local language upon request
- Must provide a copy of all loan documents to the borrower

## Digital Payment Protections (UPI / NEFT / RTGS)
- Failed UPI transactions: Money must be credited back within **T+1 business day**
- NEFT/RTGS failed transactions: Refund within 2 hours
- If bank delays refund, customer entitled to compensation at **RBI's savings deposit rate** per day

## KYC Rights
- Banks cannot refuse basic banking services solely on grounds of incomplete KYC if customer provides alternate valid documents
- Periodic KYC update is mandatory but banks must give at least 3 months notice and cannot freeze account without proper notice
- Aadhaar-based KYC (e-KYC) is valid and sufficient for most banking purposes
