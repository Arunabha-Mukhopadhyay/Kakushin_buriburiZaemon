# RBI Regulations on Loans — Borrower Rights and Fair Lending

## Key Fact Statement (KFS) — Mandatory from April 2024
The Reserve Bank of India mandated Key Fact Statements for all new retail and MSME loans from April 1, 2024. This one-page document must be provided **before loan disbursement** and must clearly state:

- Annual Percentage Rate (APR) — the true cost of the loan including all fees
- Total interest payable over the loan tenure
- All fees: processing fee, documentation fee, insurance, prepayment charges
- EMI amount and total number of EMIs
- Recovery and grievance officer contact details

**Your right:** If a lender does not give you a KFS, you can complain to RBI Ombudsman.

---

## Prepayment Rights

### Floating Rate Loans
Per RBI circular (RBI/2011-12/540), banks and NBFCs **cannot charge prepayment penalty** on floating rate loans to individual borrowers. This applies to:
- Home loans (floating rate)
- Personal loans (floating rate)
- Vehicle loans (floating rate)

### Fixed Rate Loans
Banks may charge prepayment penalty on fixed rate loans, but must disclose it clearly in the KFS/loan agreement.

---

## Annual Percentage Rate (APR) Disclosure
- All lenders must quote the effective annual interest rate (APR), not just the flat monthly rate
- A "1.5% per month flat" rate is actually ~32% APR — lenders must not hide this
- Compare loans using APR, not headline interest rate

## Credit Score and CIBIL Rights

### What is a Credit Score?
A 3-digit number (300–900) reflecting your creditworthiness based on repayment history, credit utilization, loan types, and enquiry history. Main bureaus in India:
- **CIBIL (TransUnion CIBIL)** — most widely used
- Experian India
- Equifax India
- CRIF High Mark

### Free Annual Credit Report
Every Indian is entitled to **one free credit report per year** from each bureau:
- CIBIL: myscore.cibil.com
- Experian: experian.in

### Dispute a Wrong Entry
If your credit report has an error (e.g., a loan you didn't take, a payment marked missed that you made):
1. Raise a dispute on the bureau's website
2. Bureau has 30 days to investigate and resolve
3. If not resolved: Complain to RBI Ombudsman

### What Damages Your Credit Score
- Missing EMI payments (even one 30-day delay)
- Defaulting on credit card minimum payment
- Taking multiple loans simultaneously (hard enquiries)
- High credit card utilization (using >30% of credit limit)
- Closing old credit accounts (reduces credit history length)

## Recovery Agent Regulations
Banks and NBFCs can use recovery agents for loan defaults, but agents must follow RBI guidelines:
- Must not harass, threaten, or abuse borrowers or their family members
- Must not contact borrowers before 7:00 AM or after 7:00 PM
- Must identify themselves and their lender affiliation
- Must not visit workplaces without prior permission
- Lender must provide borrower with recovery agent's contact details on request

**If violated:** File complaint with your bank's grievance officer, then RBI Ombudsman, then Banking Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in.

---

## Moratorium and Restructuring Rights
Under specific RBI guidelines (issued during COVID and other stress periods), borrowers may apply for:
- **Loan moratorium** — temporary pause on EMI payments
- **Restructuring** — extension of tenure to reduce EMI
- **One-time settlement (OTS)** — negotiated settlement for stressed borrowers

These are not always available and depend on current RBI circulars. Check rbi.org.in for current guidelines.

---

## NBFC vs Bank — Know the Difference
- **Banks** (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.) are regulated by RBI under the Banking Regulation Act
- **NBFCs** (Bajaj Finance, Muthoot, etc.) are also RBI-regulated but have different rules
- **Unregistered apps/lenders** — ILLEGAL. Check RBI's approved NBFC list before borrowing.

RBI registered NBFC list: rbi.org.in > Regulated Entities > NBFCs
