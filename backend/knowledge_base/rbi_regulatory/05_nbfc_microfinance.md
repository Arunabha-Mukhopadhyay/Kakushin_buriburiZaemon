# RBI Regulations on NBFCs and Microfinance — Protecting Borrowers

## What is an NBFC?
Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) is a financial institution registered with RBI that provides lending and investment services but cannot accept demand deposits (like a savings account). NBFCs include:
- Consumer finance companies (Bajaj Finance, Tata Capital)
- Housing finance companies (HDFC, LIC Housing Finance)
- Microfinance institutions (MFIs)
- Peer-to-peer lending platforms

## RBI Registration — Mandatory Check
Before borrowing from any NBFC or digital lender, verify their RBI registration:
- **RBI website:** rbi.org.in → "Regulated Entities" → "NBFCs" → Search by company name
- **Sachet portal:** sachet.rbi.org.in — specifically for reporting unregistered entities and checking registration

**If not on the list: DO NOT borrow.** Unregistered lenders have no obligation to follow RBI's fair practice guidelines.

## Microfinance Regulations (Updated 2022)

### Who Are MFIs?
Microfinance Institutions provide small collateral-free loans (₹5,000–₹3 lakh) primarily to low-income women borrowers in rural and semi-urban areas, typically through Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).

### RBI's Microfinance Framework (March 2022 — Key Rules)
1. **Income limit:** MFI loans available to households with annual income up to ₹3 lakh
2. **Loan cap:** Total microfinance indebtedness of a borrower (across all MFIs) cannot exceed ₹3 lakh
3. **Maximum EMI rule:** Total EMI burden cannot exceed 50% of household income — MFI must assess this before disbursing
4. **Interest rate:** No RBI-mandated cap, but MFI must disclose the effective APR clearly. RBI monitors for predatory pricing.
5. **Repayment frequency:** Borrower can choose weekly, fortnightly, or monthly — MFI cannot force weekly
6. **Prepayment:** No prepayment penalty on microfinance loans
7. **Recovery practices:** No coercive practices; agents cannot gather at borrower's home; no public humiliation

### Joint Liability Group (JLG) — How It Works
- 4–10 women borrowers form a group
- Each member guarantees the others' loans
- If one member defaults, group members are responsible
- This is NOT illegal but the responsibility must be clearly explained before joining

## Digital Lending Guidelines (RBI 2022)

### Key Rules for All Digital Lenders
1. **Loan agreement:** Must be digital and signed before disbursal; copy sent to borrower's registered email/WhatsApp
2. **Funds flow:** Loan must be disbursed directly to borrower's bank account — NOT through a fintech intermediary's wallet
3. **Repayment:** Must go directly to the lender — NOT through a third-party wallet
4. **Key Fact Statement (KFS):** Mandatory before every digital loan
5. **Cooling-off period:** Borrowers can exit a digital loan within 3 days (for short-term loans) by repaying principal + pro-rated interest; no penalty

### App Permissions — What Is Allowed
RBI directed digital lending apps to:
- NOT access contacts, camera, microphone, or location beyond minimal requirements for onboarding
- NOT store/use contacts or photos for recovery

Any app that accessed contacts for harassment purposes was ordered to be deactivated by RBI.

## Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Lending Regulation
P2P lending platforms (like Faircent, LiquiLoans) are registered as NBFC-P2P with RBI:
- **Lender limit:** Maximum ₹50 lakh across all P2P platforms
- **Borrower limit:** Maximum ₹10 lakh across all P2P platforms
- **Loan tenure:** Maximum 36 months
- P2P platforms cannot guarantee returns to lenders — returns depend on borrower repayment

## Comparison: NBFC vs Bank Loans

| Feature | Bank | NBFC |
|---|---|---|
| Can accept deposits | Yes | No (except deposits from public for specific NBFCs) |
| RBI oversight | Strict | Strict but different rules |
| Processing speed | Slower | Faster |
| Flexibility | Lower | Higher |
| Interest rates | Generally lower | Generally higher |
| Collateral required | Often yes | Often no (especially MFIs) |

## Red Flags of Unregistered Lenders
- Not listed on RBI's NBFC list
- No physical address or registered office
- Loan offer through WhatsApp/SMS only
- No loan agreement provided
- Accesses your contacts upon app install
- Disbursement to a wallet, not your bank account
- Threatens to contact your employer or family before actual default

**Report:** sachet.rbi.org.in | 1930 | cybercrime.gov.in
