# PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) and PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) — Affordable Insurance

## Overview
These two government-backed insurance schemes provide life and accident insurance at extremely low premiums, specifically designed for India's unbanked and underinsured population.

---

## PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — Accident Insurance

### What It Covers
- Death due to accident: **₹2 lakh**
- Permanent total disability (loss of both eyes/hands/feet): **₹2 lakh**
- Permanent partial disability (loss of one eye/hand/foot): **₹1 lakh**
- Does NOT cover death due to illness or natural causes

### Premium
**₹20 per year** (auto-debited from bank account annually in June)

### Eligibility
- Age: **18 to 70 years**
- Must have a savings bank account (PMJDY account valid)
- Aadhaar linked to bank account preferred

### How to Enroll
- Visit your bank branch and fill enrollment form
- Or enroll via your bank's mobile app / net banking
- Or through Common Service Centre (CSC)

### Exclusions
- Suicide or self-inflicted injury
- Accidental death under influence of alcohol/drugs
- Accident during criminal activity

---

## PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) — Life Insurance

### What It Covers
- Death due to **any cause** (illness, accident, natural causes): **₹2 lakh**
- Pure term life insurance — no maturity benefit if you survive the policy period

### Premium
**₹436 per year** (auto-debited from bank account annually in June)

### Eligibility
- Age: **18 to 50 years** (coverage continues till age 55)
- Must have a savings bank account
- Must give consent for auto-debit

### How to Enroll
- Bank branch, bank mobile app, or CSC

---

## Why These Schemes Are Critical for Target Users

### For a Gig Worker Earning ₹15,000/Month
Combined annual premium: ₹20 + ₹436 = **₹456/year** (₹38/month)

For ₹38/month, the family gets:
- ₹2 lakh in case of accidental death (PMSBY)
- ₹2 lakh in case of death from any cause (PMJJBY)
- Total coverage: **₹4 lakh** for less than the cost of a mobile recharge

### For Daily Wage / Construction Workers
A construction worker dying in a workplace accident leaves the family with zero income. ₹2 lakh from PMSBY covers 13+ months of income — enough time for the family to stabilize.

### Comparison to Private Term Insurance
A private term insurance plan for a 25-year-old for ₹50 lakh costs approximately ₹8,000–₹12,000/year. PMJJBY gives ₹2 lakh for ₹436/year — a good starting point for those who cannot afford private term insurance yet.

---

## Combined Enrollment
You can and should enroll in BOTH PMSBY and PMJJBY simultaneously:
- Same bank account
- Different auto-debit lines (₹20 for PMSBY + ₹436 for PMJJBY)
- Total annual outgo: ₹456

---

## Claim Process
1. Inform the bank immediately after death/accident
2. Submit: Death certificate (for PMJJBY), FIR + medical report (for PMSBY accident)
3. Bank processes claim with the insurer (LIC for PMJJBY, general insurers for PMSBY)
4. Claim settlement within 30 days
