# Additional Government Schemes — NPS, PM Kisan, SVANidhi, Stand Up India

---

## National Pension System (NPS) — For Formal Sector Workers

### What It Is
NPS is a voluntary, defined-contribution pension system regulated by PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority). Unlike APY (which guarantees a fixed pension), NPS returns are market-linked.

### Who Should Use NPS?
- Salaried employees (Tier-I NPS with employer contribution is mandatory for government employees)
- Self-employed / business owners who want tax-advantaged retirement savings beyond PPF
- Those who want higher potential returns than PPF (with market risk)

### Tax Benefits
- **Section 80CCD(1):** Up to ₹1.5 lakh deduction (within overall 80C limit)
- **Section 80CCD(1B):** Additional ₹50,000 deduction — EXCLUSIVE to NPS (over and above 80C limit)
- **Section 80CCD(2):** Employer contribution up to 10% of salary — tax-free (for salaried)

Combined NPS benefit: Up to ₹2 lakh tax deduction vs ₹1.5 lakh for most 80C instruments.

### Returns
- Equity option (E): 10–12% CAGR historically (market-linked)
- Corporate bonds (C): 8–9%
- Government bonds (G): 7–8%
- Auto Choice: Automatically adjusts equity/debt ratio based on age

### Key Restriction
- Minimum 80% of corpus must be used to purchase an annuity at age 60
- 20% can be withdrawn tax-free as lump sum
- Partial withdrawal allowed for specific reasons (children's education, serious illness, home purchase)

## PM Kisan Samman Nidhi — Income Support for Farmers

### What It Is
₹6,000 per year cash income support to all landholding farmer families, paid in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each directly to their bank account.

### Eligibility
- All landholding farmer families (land in their name)
- Both small (< 2 hectares) and large landholders (scheme expanded in 2019)
- Exclusions: Government employees, income tax payers, institutional landholders, constitutional post holders

### How to Register
- Visit nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) with land records (Khasra/Khatauni) and Aadhaar
- Online: pmkisan.gov.in → "New Farmer Registration"

### Check Payment Status
pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status" → Enter Aadhaar or mobile

## PM SVANidhi — Working Capital for Street Vendors

### What It Is
PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi — provides collateral-free working capital loans to street vendors whose livelihoods were disrupted.

### Loan Structure
| Tier | Loan Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1st loan | ₹10,000 | No collateral, simple application |
| 2nd loan | ₹20,000 | Repaid 1st loan on time |
| 3rd loan | ₹50,000 | Repaid 2nd loan on time |

### Benefits
- **Interest subsidy:** 7% annual interest subsidy credited to bank account quarterly
- **Digital incentive:** ₹1,200/year cashback for digital transactions via UPI
- **Credit score benefit:** Timely repayment reported to credit bureaus — helps build credit history

### Eligibility
- Street vendors operating on or before March 24, 2020
- Must have Certificate of Vending OR Letter of Recommendation from Urban Local Body (ULB)

### How to Apply
- pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
- Nearest Urban Local Body office

## Stand Up India — Loans for SC/ST and Women Entrepreneurs

### What It Is
Provides bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch for setting up greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, or trading.

### Eligibility
- SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs
- Above 18 years of age
- Enterprise should be a greenfield project (first venture)
- Non-individual enterprises must have 51%+ SC/ST or women shareholding

### Loan Features
- Loan: ₹10 lakh – ₹1 crore (composite loan covering term loan + working capital)
- Repayment: Maximum 7 years
- Moratorium: Up to 18 months
- Covered under Credit Guarantee Scheme for Stand Up India Loans (CGSSIUA) — no personal collateral required

### How to Apply
standupmitra.in | Nearest bank branch (all scheduled commercial banks participate)

## PM Vishwakarma — Support for Traditional Artisans

### What It Is (Launched 2023)
Support scheme for 18 traditional trades (carpenters, cobblers, potters, blacksmiths, weavers, goldsmiths, etc.)

### Benefits
- **Recognition:** PM Vishwakarma certificate and ID card
- **Credit:** Collateral-free loan — ₹1 lakh (Tier 1), ₹2 lakh (Tier 2) at 5% interest (government subsidizes to 5% from market rate)
- **Skill training:** 5-7 days basic training with ₹500/day stipend; advanced training available
- **Toolkit support:** ₹15,000 toolkit grant
- **Digital payment incentive:** Up to ₹100/day for 100 days for digital transactions

### Eligibility
- Traditional artisan or craftsperson in one of the 18 trades
- Working with hands/tools
- Not enrolled in PMEGP, PM Mudra, or Stand Up India

### How to Apply
pmvishwakarma.gov.in | CSC centres | nearest bank branch
