# Atal Pension Yojana (APY) — Guaranteed Pension for Unorganised Workers

## Overview
Atal Pension Yojana (APY) was launched on June 1, 2015 under the National Pension System (NPS) framework. It is specifically designed for workers in the **unorganised sector** — gig workers, domestic workers, daily wage workers, street vendors, small shop owners — who have no employer-provided pension.

## What You Get
APY guarantees a **fixed monthly pension after the age of 60**, for life. The pension amount depends on how much you contribute and when you join.

### Pension Tiers

| Monthly Pension at 60 | Monthly Contribution (joining at 18) | Monthly Contribution (joining at 40) |
|---|---|---|
| ₹1,000/month | ₹42 | ₹291 |
| ₹2,000/month | ₹84 | ₹582 |
| ₹3,000/month | ₹126 | ₹873 |
| ₹4,000/month | ₹168 | ₹1,164 |
| ₹5,000/month | ₹210 | ₹1,454 |

**Key insight:** Joining early makes a massive difference. Someone joining at 18 contributes only ₹210/month for a ₹5,000/month pension, while someone joining at 40 contributes ₹1,454/month for the same pension.

## Eligibility
- Indian citizen aged **18 to 40 years**
- Must have an active savings bank account (PMJDY account is valid)
- Must have a mobile number linked to the account
- **Not eligible if you are or were an income tax payer** (rule updated from October 2022)

## How to Enroll
1. Visit your bank branch (any nationalized bank, private bank, small finance bank, or post office)
2. Fill APY enrollment form
3. Provide Aadhaar and mobile number
4. Set up auto-debit from savings account (monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly)

Or enroll online through:
- Your bank's net banking / mobile app
- NPS portal: npscra.nsdl.co.in

## Benefits at Death
- If subscriber dies before 60: Spouse can continue the account until 60 and receive the same pension, OR withdraw the full accumulated corpus
- If subscriber dies after 60: Spouse receives the same monthly pension for life; after both die, the nominee receives the accumulated corpus

## Tax Benefit
Contributions qualify for deduction under **Section 80CCD(1)** — within the overall ₹1.5 lakh limit of Section 80C.

## Why APY Is Critical for Gig Workers
Gig economy workers (Zomato/Swiggy delivery, Ola/Uber drivers, daily wage earners) have:
- No Provident Fund (EPF)
- No gratuity
- No employer pension contribution

APY is currently the **only structured, government-guaranteed pension** available to this segment. Even ₹42/month from age 18 secures ₹1,000/month pension for life — better than nothing.

## Contribution Default
If auto-debit fails due to insufficient balance:
- ₹1 per month penalty for every ₹100 of due contribution
- Account frozen after 6 months of non-payment; closed after 24 months
- Defaulters cannot re-enroll
