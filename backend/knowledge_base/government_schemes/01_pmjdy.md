# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is India's national financial inclusion mission launched on August 28, 2014. It aims to provide universal access to banking facilities to every household in India.

## Key Benefits
- **Zero balance savings account** at any bank branch or Business Correspondent (BC) outlet
- **RuPay Debit Card** with accident insurance cover of ₹2 lakh (for accounts opened after August 28, 2018)
- **Overdraft facility** of up to ₹10,000 (available after satisfactory account operation for 6 months; for accounts held by women, the limit is ₹10,000)
- **Life insurance cover** of ₹30,000 for account holders who opened accounts between August 15, 2014 and January 26, 2015
- **Interest on deposits**
- **Access to Pension, Insurance Products**
- **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** — government subsidies and scheme benefits are credited directly to this account

## Eligibility
- Any Indian citizen aged 10 years or above
- No minimum balance requirement
- Can be opened with Aadhaar card or any official valid document

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card (preferred — enables instant account opening)
- OR: Voter ID / Driving Licence / PAN Card / NREGA job card / passport

## Where to Open
- Any nationalized bank branch
- Post office savings bank
- Business Correspondent (Bank Mitra) in rural areas
- Some accounts can be opened via mobile banking apps

## Overdraft Facility — Conditions
- Account must be at least 6 months old
- Account must show regular transactions
- Overdraft up to ₹10,000 is available; first ₹2,000 without any conditions
- Only one account per household is eligible for overdraft

## Why This Matters for ArthSaathi Users
PMJDY is often the entry point to formal banking for first-time earners and gig workers. Having a PMJDY account enables:
- Receiving salary / payment via bank transfer
- Getting government DBT benefits (PM Kisan, LPG subsidy, etc.)
- Building a 6-month transaction history needed for formal loans
- Accessing RuPay card for digital payments

## Current Status (as of 2025)
Over 53 crore (530 million) accounts opened under PMJDY. Total deposits exceed ₹2.3 lakh crore. Scheme is ongoing with no end date declared.
