# PM Awas Yojana (PMAY) — Affordable Housing for Urban and Rural Poor

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is India's flagship affordable housing scheme launched in 2015. It provides financial assistance to low and middle-income individuals to build or buy a home.

**Two components:**
1. **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U):** For people living in urban areas (cities, towns)
2. **PMAY-Gramin / Rural (PMAY-G):** For people living in rural areas

---

## PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U)

### Who Can Apply? (Beneficiary Categories)
| Category | Annual Household Income | Subsidy |
|---|---|---|
| EWS (Economically Weaker Section) | Up to ₹3 lakh | ₹2.67 lakh (6.5% interest subsidy on ₹6 lakh loan for 20 years) |
| LIG (Low Income Group) | ₹3 lakh – ₹6 lakh | ₹2.67 lakh (same as EWS) |
| MIG-I (Middle Income Group I) | ₹6 lakh – ₹12 lakh | ₹2.35 lakh (4% subsidy on ₹9 lakh loan) |
| MIG-II (Middle Income Group II) | ₹12 lakh – ₹18 lakh | ₹2.30 lakh (3% subsidy on ₹12 lakh loan) |

### Key Conditions
- Applicant or any family member must **NOT own a pucca house** anywhere in India
- First-time home buyer only
- For EWS/LIG: Female co-ownership mandatory (house must be in wife's name or jointly)
- Property must be in an urban area (city/town with urban local body)

### How Subsidy Works
The interest subsidy is credited upfront to your loan account, reducing your EMI. Example for EWS:
- Home loan: ₹10 lakh at 8.5% for 20 years → EMI: ₹8,678
- After ₹2.67 lakh subsidy credited to principal → Effective loan: ₹7.33 lakh → EMI: ₹6,354
- Monthly saving: ₹2,324

### Where to Apply
- Bank/housing finance company branch (SBI, LIC Housing Finance, HDFC, etc.)
- Official portal: pmaymis.gov.in
- Common Service Centres (CSC) in rural areas

## PMAY-Gramin (Rural)

### Who Is Eligible?
Based on SECC 2011 data (Socio-Economic Caste Census):
- Houseless families
- Families living in zero, one, or two room kutcha (non-pucca) house
- SC/ST families, freed bonded labourers, minorities (in specific states)

### Assistance Amount
- **Plains:** ₹1.20 lakh per house
- **Hilly/difficult terrain (Northeast, J&K, etc.):** ₹1.30 lakh per house
- Additional ₹12,000 from Swachh Bharat Mission for toilet construction
- Additional ₹18,000 under MGNREGA for 90 person-days of unskilled labour

### Payment Method
Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) in installments as construction progresses. Verified by geotagged photos uploaded by local gram panchayat official.

### Check Eligibility and Status
- pmayg.nic.in → "Stakeholders" → "IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"
- Enter your registration number or Aadhaar to check beneficiary status

## Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana 2.0 (2024–2029)
The government announced PMAY 2.0 to continue the scheme. Key additions:
- Target: 1 crore additional urban homes
- Enhanced focus on rental housing for migrant workers
- Technology-driven monitoring via geo-tagging and digital verification

## Common Issues and Solutions
| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| Name not in SECC list despite eligibility | Apply through gram panchayat / ward office for manual verification |
| Subsidy not credited despite disbursement | Check CLSS tracker at pmaymis.gov.in → "Citizen Assessment" |
| Agent demanding fee to process application | Report immediately — all PMAY applications are FREE |
| Construction stalled due to fund delay | Contact local PMAY district coordinator or helpline |
