# Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) — Loans for Small Businesses

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) was launched on April 8, 2015 to provide affordable credit to micro and small enterprises, especially those in the non-corporate, non-farm sector. "MUDRA" stands for Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency.

## Who Can Apply?
- Proprietorship firms, partnership firms, small manufacturing units
- Shopkeepers, vegetable vendors, fruit sellers, artisans, food vendors
- Service sector units (salons, repair shops, tailoring, etc.)
- Self Help Group (SHG) members
- Gig economy workers with a business activity
- Any individual or entity engaged in income-generating activity in manufacturing, trading, or services

**NOT eligible:** Farmers (they have separate Kisan Credit Card scheme), corporate entities

## Three Loan Categories

### 1. Shishu — Up to ₹50,000
- For businesses at early/startup stage
- Minimal documentation
- No collateral required
- Typical repayment: 3–5 years

### 2. Kishore — ₹50,001 to ₹5 Lakh
- For established businesses needing expansion capital
- Basic financial statements may be required
- No mandatory collateral (covered under Credit Guarantee Fund)

### 3. Tarun — ₹5 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh
- For businesses with proven track record
- Financial statements, IT returns, business plan required
- No mandatory collateral for loans up to ₹10 lakh under CGTMSE

**New Category (2024):** Tarun Plus — ₹10 Lakh to ₹20 Lakh for well-established businesses that have already repaid Tarun loans.

## Interest Rates
- No fixed rate — each bank / NBFC / MFI sets its own rate
- Typically ranges from 9% to 12% per annum for formal banks
- Microfinance institutions may charge higher (regulated by RBI)
- No processing fee mandated (varies by lender)

## How to Apply
1. **Online:** udyamimitra.in (official MUDRA portal)
2. **Offline:** Visit nearest public sector bank branch, private bank, Regional Rural Bank (RRB), Microfinance Institution (MFI), or Small Finance Bank
3. Fill MUDRA loan application form
4. Submit documents

## Documents Required (varies by loan amount)
- Identity proof: Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID
- Address proof
- Business proof: GST registration, Udyam registration, shop license, or self-declaration
- Bank statements (6 months for Kishore and Tarun)
- Photographs
- Quotation of equipment/machinery (if applicable)

## MUDRA Card
All MUDRA borrowers get a **MUDRA Card** — a RuPay debit card linked to a working capital account. This allows withdrawal of funds as needed rather than in lump sum, reducing interest burden.

## Key Advantage
Loans under PMMY are covered under the **Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)** — meaning the government provides a guarantee to the lender. This is why banks can lend without collateral. The borrower does not need to arrange property/gold as security.
