# Insurance in India — What Every First-Time Earner Must Know

## Why Insurance Comes Before Investment
The purpose of insurance is to protect your financial plan from being destroyed by a catastrophic event. Without adequate insurance:
- One serious illness can wipe out years of savings
- One accidental death can leave dependents with no income
- All your SIPs and PPF contributions mean nothing if a medical bill forces you to liquidate them

**Rule of thumb: Fund your emergency fund first, then get insured, then invest.**

---

## 1. Term Life Insurance

### What It Is
Pure life insurance — pays a large lump sum (sum assured) to your nominee if you die during the policy term. No investment component, no maturity benefit if you survive.

### Why Term > Traditional (Endowment/ULIP)
Traditional plans mix insurance with investment and deliver poor results at both. ₹10,000/month in an endowment plan buys ₹15 lakh of cover. The same ₹1,000/month in a term plan buys ₹1 crore of cover. Invest the remaining ₹9,000 separately in mutual funds — you'll be wealthier and better covered.

### How Much Cover Do You Need?
Standard formula: **10–15 times your annual income**
- Annual income ₹3,60,000 → Cover needed: ₹36 lakh – ₹54 lakh (minimum ₹50 lakh recommended)

### What to Look For
- **Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR):** Higher is better. Look for >97%. LIC CSR ~98%, HDFC Life ~99%, Max Life ~99%
- **Solvency Ratio:** Must be above 1.5 (regulatory minimum). A measure of insurer's financial strength.
- **Online plans are cheaper** than agent-sold plans (no commission)

### Approximate Premiums (₹1 crore cover, 30-year term)
| Age | Annual Premium (approx.) |
|---|---|
| 25 years | ₹8,000 – ₹10,000 |
| 30 years | ₹11,000 – ₹14,000 |
| 35 years | ₹16,000 – ₹22,000 |
| 40 years | ₹25,000 – ₹35,000 |

**Buy early. Premium is locked at the age you buy.**

## 2. Health Insurance

### Why It Is Non-Negotiable
Average hospitalization costs in India (private hospitals):
- Appendix surgery: ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000
- Angioplasty: ₹2 lakh – ₹5 lakh
- Cancer treatment: ₹5 lakh – ₹30 lakh+

A single hospitalization can wipe out years of savings for a middle-income family.

### Minimum Recommended Cover
- Individual: ₹5 lakh (₹10 lakh in metros)
- Family floater (covers spouse + children): ₹10 lakh – ₹15 lakh

### Key Terms to Understand
- **Premium:** Annual payment to keep insurance active
- **Sum Insured:** Maximum hospital bill covered per policy year
- **Deductible/Co-pay:** Portion of the bill you pay; rest covered by insurer
- **Waiting Period:** Most plans have 2–4 year waiting period for pre-existing diseases
- **Network hospitals:** Hospitals where you can get cashless treatment (insurer pays hospital directly)

### Government Health Insurance Options
- **Ayushman Bharat — PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY):** Free health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for below-poverty-line families. Check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in
- **State schemes:** Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (Maharashtra), Arogyashri (Andhra/Telangana), Bhamashah (Rajasthan) — check state health department

## 3. Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) — Free Health Insurance

### Who Is Eligible?
Based on SECC (Socio-Economic Caste Census) 2011 data. Eligible if:
- Household is in the lowest economic quintile
- Scheduled caste/tribe households
- Landless agricultural laborers, construction workers, domestic workers, ragpickers, beggars, tribal populations
- Rural families with specific criteria (no pucca house, no adult earning member, manual scavengers, etc.)

### Coverage
- **₹5 lakh per family per year**
- Covers 1,929 medical procedures (surgery, hospitalization, follow-up)
- Cashless at any empaneled government or private hospital
- No premium — completely free for eligible families
- No restriction on family size

### How to Check Eligibility
- Visit pmjay.gov.in → "Am I Eligible?" → enter mobile or Aadhaar
- Visit nearest Ayushman Mitra or government hospital help desk
- Call helpline: 14555

## 4. Insurance Fraud — What to Watch For
- **Fake insurance agents:** Always verify agent license on IRDAI portal (irdai.gov.in > Insurance Intermediaries)
- **Policy switching scams:** Agents pressure you to surrender existing policy and buy new one (they earn commission; you lose surrender value and restart waiting periods)
- **Fake claim rejection:** Insurer rejecting valid claim on technical grounds — raise complaint on IRDAI's IGMS portal (igms.irda.gov.in)

**IRDAI Helpline:** 155255 | **Complaint Portal:** igms.irda.gov.in
